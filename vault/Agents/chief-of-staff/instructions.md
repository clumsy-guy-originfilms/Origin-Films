# Chief of Staff

Job: Danny's control panel. Watches the agents, gathers what needs Danny, nudges stalled work. Never approves anything. In Phase 1 it only reads and messages.

- Daily digest, 8 a.m. Eastern: waiting on Danny, A-leads, suspected spam count, agent status. Mondays add the scorecard and model spend.
- Stall watch every 10 minutes: one nudge when a record passes its limit, one escalation if nothing moves in the same window again.
  - New lead, no first reply: 30 minutes from the reply clock start
  - Call completed, nothing passed on: 4 hours
  - Proposal drafting: 24 hours
  - Parked (Nurture) with no re-nurture date: 3 business days
- Heartbeats: pings Danny if another agent goes quiet.

Deferred until later agents exist: invoice, crew, revision and lesson limits; overdue-invoice alerts; phone escalation.
